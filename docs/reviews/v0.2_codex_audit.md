# v0.2 Codex Repository Audit for Independent Aerospace-Dynamics Review

**Audit target:** `6640e6d9826f1accb76e084773986b759235b842` (`6640e6d`)  
**Audit date:** 2026-08-30  
**Scope:** Repository-level trace of the currently documented prescribed-kinematic v0.2 model. No physics formulation is selected or changed by this audit.

## Executive summary

The implementation is internally consistent as a **prescribed kinematic calculation**: it deterministically applies the declared reel schedule, mass map, fixed analytic CM motion, co-rotating-frame velocity field, and endpoint tension estimators. Its mass, geometry, CM reconstruction, and numerical angular-momentum residual calculations agree with its implemented arithmetic.

It is **not internally consistent with a closed-system Earth-centered angular-momentum conservation claim** when tether length changes. The fixed circular CM path and fixed \(\omega\) combine with the specified \(\omega\times\rho\) term to increase the component rotational angular momentum by \(\omega\Delta I_{cm}\). The implementation correctly exposes, rather than fixes, this conflict. It does not define a closed system because it omits reel/drum inertia, actuator/reaction torque, attitude states, actuator/orbit transfer, and extended-body CM response.

Software correctness, numerical verification, model consistency, scientific validation, and independent aerospace review are distinct. Passing tests or a passing numerical residual is not evidence that the model is physically valid.

## Angular-momentum system boundary

### Included in the implementation

- **Reference point:** Earth center, via inertial ECI vectors \(r_i\times v_i\).
- **Upper body:** `upper_mass` at the upper tip.
- **Lower body and undeployed tether:** `lower_effective_mass_kg` at the lower tip, combining lower end mass with \(\rho(L_f-\ell)\).
- **Deployed tether:** `tether_deployed_mass_kg` at the midpoint state.
- **Translational/orbital contribution:** implicit in every component’s full Earth-centered position and velocity; analytically it is \(M R\times V\).
- **Relative rotational contribution:** supplied by `omega × signed_offset × r_hat`, giving \(I_{cm}\omega\).
- **Radial payout contribution:** supplied by signed offset rates; it has no z-angular-momentum contribution in the implemented planar radial geometry.

### Excluded or not defined

- Reel/drum rotational inertia and state.
- Actuator shaft torque and reaction torque.
- Spacecraft attitude and reaction-wheel momentum.
- An actuator/orbit angular-momentum ledger.
- Any external torque definition.
- Extended-body CM acceleration and orbital response to differential gravity.
- A mass-transfer momentum flux model at the reel/tether interface.

Therefore the implementation does **not** define a closed system sufficient to assert that the modeled component \(H\) must be conserved during changing-inertia co-rotation.

## \(\omega\Delta I\) discrepancy audit

For the implemented planar field, the ADR derivation gives:

\[
H=M R\times V+I_{cm}(\ell)\omega,
\qquad
I_{cm}=m_u s_u^2+m_l^{eff}s_l^2+m_t s_t^2.
\]

With fixed circular \(R\) and \(\omega=n\), the discrepancy is:

\[
\Delta H=\omega[I_{cm}(\ell)-I_{cm}(\ell_0)].
\]

Reproduced benchmark residuals are below. `ωΔI` agrees with observed `ΔH` to floating-point rounding, as recorded in ADR-002. This demonstrates that the numerical discrepancy follows the imposed kinematic relationship. It does **not** demonstrate that either the prescribed field or an unmodeled actuator/orbit exchange is physically valid.

## Fixed-CM / changing-length analysis

- **Kinematic consistency:** The code consistently evaluates the stated algebraic offsets and prescribed ECI velocity field from the analytic CM state.
- **Numerical consistency:** The mass map, CM reconstruction, tether separation, radial alignment, and \(H\) residual are calculated deterministically from the generated samples.
- **Conservation consistency:** Not closed-system consistent: fixed \(MR^2\omega\) plus changing \(I_{cm}\omega\) cannot preserve total component \(H\) for nonzero \(\Delta I_{cm}\).
- **Physical/dynamical consistency:** Not established. The fixed CM is a declared simplification, but it excludes dynamics at the same order required to reconcile the rotating-inertia angular-momentum change.

If closed-system constrained dynamics were intended, the repository would need, at minimum: equations for all component accelerations under differential central gravity; explicit constraint/reel forces and torques; momentum flux at tether payout; reel/drum and/or attitude angular-momentum states; an equation for CM/orbital response; an integration method; and tension equations derived for those states. This audit does not provide or choose those equations.

## Mass transfer, reel, actuator, and tension audit

The mass convention moves \(\rho\ell\) from an undeployed mass collocated with the lower body to a deployed point mass at the tether midpoint. Offset rates account for the changing algebraic CM placement, and the midpoint receives the §8 co-rotating plus radial velocity field.

The implementation does not specify the velocity at which material crosses the reel boundary, a momentum flux, reel/drum inertia, actuator torque, or reaction torque. Accordingly, it does not dynamically couple tension to an actuator. `estimate_tension` is a post-kinematic endpoint free-body calculation: it reports upper and lower inferred dynamic values that may differ when tether mass is present. The implementation labels these as engineering estimates, not material allowables.

An aerospace reviewer must resolve whether the reel acts as an external prescribed boundary or an included dynamic component, define the sign convention and momentum carried by transferred mass, and specify whether tension is a constraint reaction, actuator load, or a separately modeled distributed force.
