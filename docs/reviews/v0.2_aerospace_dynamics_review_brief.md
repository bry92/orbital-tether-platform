## A. Model purpose and boundaries

v0.2 is a prescribed finite-time, radially constrained tether-deployment research model. Given a circular initial orbit, a commanded payout schedule, fixed end masses, and optional uniform tether linear density, it produces time histories of component states and engineering tension estimates. It retains the v0.1 instantaneous API separately.

It does **not** model flexible tether dynamics, libration, skip-rope, elasticity, shock, reel/drum inertia, actuator torque, attitude response, drag, J2, third-body gravity, SRP, electrodynamics, momentum-exchange operations, mission optimization, safety margins, or flight software. Tension outputs are engineering estimates under enforced kinematics, not structural allowables or validated load cases.

## B. Current equations and stated conservation claim

The binding v0.2 specification uses an analytic circular CM path (§7):

\[
\theta=nt,\qquad
\mathbf r_{cm}=r[\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0]^T,\qquad
\mathbf v_{cm}=nr[-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0]^T.
\]

For radial offsets \(\boldsymbol\rho_i\), §8 assigns component positions and velocities:

\[
\mathbf r_i=\mathbf r_{cm}+\boldsymbol\rho_i,\qquad
\boldsymbol\omega=\frac{\mathbf r_{cm}\times\mathbf v_{cm}}{r^2},\qquad
\mathbf v_i=\mathbf v_{cm}+\boldsymbol\omega\times\boldsymbol\rho_i+\dot{s}_i\hat r.
\]

The mass map holds the component CM at \(\mathbf r_{cm}\). The implementation calculates Earth-centered angular momentum as the sum of all modeled component \(m_i\mathbf r_i\times\mathbf v_i\). Specification §10 states this quantity is conserved under central gravity and radial internal tension, and §11 sets a `1e-8` relative residual tolerance.

For the current fixed-CM/co-rotating field, the ADR derives instead:

\[
\mathbf H=M\mathbf R\times\mathbf V+I_{cm}(\ell)\boldsymbol\omega,
\qquad
\Delta\mathbf H=n[I_{cm}(\ell)-I_{cm}(\ell_0)]\hat z.
\]

This relationship is the subject of the unresolved review decision; no formulation has been selected.

## E. Unresolved engineering decision

The reviewer is asked to determine whether v0.2 is intended to be:

1. a **prescribed kinematic model** with an explicit actuator/orbit angular-momentum ledger; or
2. a **closed-system constrained dynamics model** with CM/orbital response.

The repository cannot responsibly choose between these interpretations. It has no reel/drum inertia, actuator torque, attitude response, actuator/orbit exchange requirement, extended-body CM equation of motion, or independent aerospace-dynamics evidence. A fixed circular CM plus co-rotating relative velocity field changes component angular momentum as inertia changes, while §10 describes closed-system conservation.
