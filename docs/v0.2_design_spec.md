# v0.2 Design Specification — Finite-Time Tether Deployment

**Document ID:** OTDT-DS-002  
**Revision:** A  
**Date:** 2026-07-16  
**Status:** Binding for implementation  
**Supersedes for deployment modeling:** instantaneous-only path of v0.1 (v0.1 API retained)

---

## 1. Purpose

Extend the Orbital Tether Digital Twin from an **instantaneous deployment map** (v0.1) to a **finite-time, radially constrained deployment** with:

1. Explicit reel / length schedule parameters  
2. Time history of tip states during deployment  
3. Tether linear density (deployed mass)  
4. Tension estimates (static gravity-gradient and dynamic / prescribed-length)  
5. Verification evidence compatible with the existing experiment-record philosophy  

**Non-goals (still out of scope):** UI, AI/ML autonomy features, marketing pages, flexible-tether PDEs, libration ODEs (deferred to v0.3), EDT, MET catch/throw.

---

## 2. Problem statement (what v0.2 answers)

> Given a spacecraft on a circular orbit that **pays out a tether over finite time** at a commanded reel rate, remaining **radially aligned** about the system CM, with optional uniform tether linear density: what are the time histories of tip states, estimated tension, and do mass and angular momentum about Earth remain consistent under the stated constraints?

v0.2 does **not** answer: deployment stability, snag loads, elasticity, skip-rope, or flight tension margins.

## 5. Reel / length schedule

### 5.1 Parameters

| Parameter | Symbol | Units | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|-------|
| `final_length_m` | \(L_f\) | m | \(> 0\) |
| `initial_length_m` | \(\ell_0\) | m | \(0 < \ell_0 \le L_f\) (numerical floor, not zero) |
| `reel_rate_m_s` | \(u_{\mathrm{cmd}}\) | m/s | Constant payout rate while extending |
| `reel_acceleration_m_s2` | \(a_{\mathrm{cmd}}\) | m/s² | Optional; default 0 (constant rate) |
| `coast_after_deploy_s` | \(t_{\mathrm{coast}}\) | s | Hold \(\ell=L_f\) after payout |

### 5.2 Default schedule (constant rate)

\[
\dot{\ell}(t) =
\begin{cases}
u_{\mathrm{cmd}} & \ell(t) < L_f \\
0 & \ell(t) \ge L_f
\end{cases}
,\qquad
\ddot{\ell}(t) = 0 \text{ except at discrete rate switches (neglected)}
\]

\[
\ell(t) = \min\bigl(\ell_0 + u_{\mathrm{cmd}}\, t,\; L_f\bigr)
\]

Deployment duration:

\[
T_{\mathrm{dep}} = \frac{L_f - \ell_0}{u_{\mathrm{cmd}}} \quad (u_{\mathrm{cmd}} > 0)
\]

**SIMPLIFIED:** Instantaneous start/stop of reel rate (no motor slew). Documented as assumption **A-014**.

## 6. Mass distribution

### 6.1 End masses and tether mass

Total system mass is conserved:

\[
m_{\mathrm{tot}} = m_u + m_l + \rho L_f
\]

At deployed length \(\ell\):

| Component | Mass | Position relative to CM |
|-----------|------|-------------------------|
| Upper end mass | \(m_u\) | \(+s_u\,\hat{r}\) |
| Lower end mass | \(m_l\) | \(-s_l\,\hat{r}\) |
| Deployed tether | \(\rho\ell\) | treated as point mass at tether midpoint |
| Undeployed tether (on reel) | \(\rho(L_f-\ell)\) | collocated with **lower** end mass (deployer) |

**SIMPLIFIED (A-013):** Deployed tether continuum replaced by a single midpoint point mass. Undeployed tether mass rides entirely on the lower body. No thermal mass transfer, no reel drum inertia.

### 6.2 Geometry (radial constraint)

Let effective lower mass include undeployed tether:

\[
m_l^{\mathrm{eff}}(\ell) = m_l + \rho(L_f - \ell)
\]

Deployed tether mass:

\[
m_t(\ell) = \rho\,\ell
\]

Require CM at \(\mathbf{r}_{cm}\) with radial offsets \(s_u, s_l > 0\) and midpoint offset \(s_t\):

\[
s_u + s_l = \ell,\qquad s_t = \frac{s_u - s_l}{2}
\]

Mass-balance about CM (scalar radial):

\[
m_u s_u + m_t s_t - m_l^{\mathrm{eff}} s_l = 0
\]

With \(s_l = \ell - s_u\) and \(s_t = s_u - \ell/2\):

\[
s_u =
\frac{
  m_l^{\mathrm{eff}}\,\ell + m_t\,(\ell/2)
}{
  m_u + m_l^{\mathrm{eff}} + m_t
}
,\qquad
s_l = \ell - s_u
\]

When \(\rho = 0\), this reduces to the v0.1 relations \(s_u = \ell\, m_l / (m_u+m_l)\), \(s_l = \ell\, m_u / (m_u+m_l)\).

**Radial alignment (A-006 retained):** tether axis forced along \(\hat{r}\). No libration degree of freedom (v0.3).

## 7. Center-of-mass motion

Under central gravity and **internal** tension forces along the tether (which lies on a line through Earth’s center when radially aligned), the CM obeys two-body dynamics:

\[
\ddot{\mathbf{r}}_{cm} = -\mu \frac{\mathbf{r}_{cm}}{|\mathbf{r}_{cm}|^3}
\]

**v0.2 integration choice:** CM remains on the **initial circular orbit** (analytic angle advance):

\[
\theta(t) = n t,\quad
\mathbf{r}_{cm}(t) = r\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta\\\sin\theta\\0\end{bmatrix},\quad
\mathbf{v}_{cm}(t) = n r\begin{bmatrix}-\sin\theta\\\cos\theta\\0\end{bmatrix}
\]

with \(r = |\mathbf{r}_{cm}(0)|\) fixed.

**SIMPLIFIED (A-015):** Neglects CM orbit perturbation from higher-multipole modeling of extended mass (point-mass Earth already; extended-body self-gravity neglected). Acceptable while \(\ell \ll r\).

## 8. Tip kinematics (finite-time)

Positions:

\[
\mathbf{r}_u = \mathbf{r}_{cm} + s_u\hat{r},\quad
\mathbf{r}_l = \mathbf{r}_{cm} - s_l\hat{r},\quad
\mathbf{r}_t = \mathbf{r}_{cm} + s_t\hat{r}
\]

Orbital angular velocity of the radial frame (circular CM):

\[
\boldsymbol{\omega} = \frac{\mathbf{r}_{cm}\times\mathbf{v}_{cm}}{r^2}
\]

Length rate partitions into offset rates via \(\dot{s}_u, \dot{s}_l\) obtained by differentiating the mass-balance expressions (analytic derivatives implemented in code).

Inertial velocities (radial constraint + payout):

\[
\mathbf{v}_i = \mathbf{v}_{cm} + \boldsymbol{\omega}\times\boldsymbol{\rho}_i + \dot{s}_i\,\hat{r}
\quad(i \in \{u,l,t\})
\]

where \(\boldsymbol{\rho}_u = s_u\hat{r}\), \(\boldsymbol{\rho}_l = -s_l\hat{r}\), \(\boldsymbol{\rho}_t = s_t\hat{r}\).

**Relation to v0.1:** Instantaneous deploy with \(\dot{s}_i = 0\) and \(\mathbf{v}_i = \mathbf{v}_{cm}\) is **not** identical to this kinematic field. v0.2 uses the constrained radial velocity field above (assumption **A-012**). The v0.1 instantaneous API remains available for regression.

## 9. Tension estimation

Two estimators are reported. Both are **engineering estimates**, not material allowables.

### 9.1 Static gravity-gradient tension (slow-deploy limit)

For co-rotating tips (\(\dot{\ell}\approx 0\), \(\ddot{\ell}\approx 0\)), free-body on the upper end mass:

\[
T_{\mathrm{static}} =
m_u\left(
  \omega^2 r_u - \frac{\mu}{r_u^2}
\right)
\]

with \(\omega = |\boldsymbol{\omega}|\), \(r_u = |\mathbf{r}_u|\).  
(Sign convention: \(T > 0\) means tension — tether in tension pulling the upper mass inward.)

Equivalent first-order check formula (\(\ell \ll r\), equal-mass reference):

\[
T_{\mathrm{gg,approx}} \approx 3 n^2 \mu_{\mathrm{red}} \ell,
\quad
\mu_{\mathrm{red}} = \frac{m_u m_l^{\mathrm{eff}}}{m_u + m_l^{\mathrm{eff}}}
\]

**SIMPLIFIED (A-016):** Ignores tether-mass distributed load; uses end-mass free-body. No bending, no elasticity.

### 9.2 Dynamic tension for prescribed length

Treat radial separation as a prescribed coordinate. Reduced-mass along-track radial EOM (circular reference, planar, radial constraint):

\[
\mu_{\mathrm{red}}\ddot{\ell}
=
\mu_{\mathrm{red}}\, a_{\mathrm{gg}}(\ell; r_{cm}, \mu)
+ \mu_{\mathrm{red}}\, a_{\mathrm{kin}}(\ell, \dot{\ell}; n)
- T_{\mathrm{dyn}}
\]

Rearranged:

\[
T_{\mathrm{dyn}}
=
\mu_{\mathrm{red}}\bigl(a_{\mathrm{gg}} + a_{\mathrm{kin}} - \ddot{\ell}\bigr)
\]

where \(a_{\mathrm{gg}}\) is the differential gravitational acceleration projected along the tether (exact two-body difference between tip unit radial accelerations, not Hill-linearized only), and \(a_{\mathrm{kin}}\) collects rotating-frame kinematic terms consistent with the constrained velocity field (centrifugal / Coriolis contributions along \(\hat{r}\)).

**Implementation approach in code:** evaluate tip gravitational accelerations in ECI, project the relative acceleration onto \(\hat{r}\), and solve for the scalar tension required so that the relative radial acceleration matches the prescribed \(\ddot{\ell}\) (and mass-map \(\ddot{s}_u - (-\ddot{s}_l)\)).

**SIMPLIFIED (A-016):** Single scalar tension; massless tether force path (tether midpoint mass feels gravity only; tension network idealized as one link between ends). No damping, no slack (\(T<0\) flagged as **slack/compression warning**, not a contact model).

## 10. Conservation laws (verification philosophy)

| Quantity | Expected under v0.2 model | Test |
|----------|---------------------------|------|
| Total mass \(m_{\mathrm{tot}}\) | Exact constant | residual = 0 |
| Angular momentum about Earth \(\mathbf{H} = \sum m_i \mathbf{r}_i\times\mathbf{v}_i\) | Conserved (central forces + radial tension through Earth line) | relative residual vs \(t=0\) |
| CM radius / circular energy of CM | Constant by construction (analytic circular) | CM \(r\) residual |
| Mechanical energy of tips | **Not** conserved: reel actuator exchanges energy | tracked as diagnostic; not a PASS gate |
| Instantaneous v0.1 \(H\) vs co-rotation | unchanged diagnostics | existing tests remain |

Slack / negative \(T_{\mathrm{dyn}}\) does not fail conservation; it fails a **tension_sign** advisory check.

## 11. Numerical method

1. Build time grid \(t_k \in [0, T_{\mathrm{dep}} + t_{\mathrm{coast}}]\) with step \(\Delta t\) (default: resolve ≥ 100 samples over deployment).  
2. At each \(t_k\): evaluate \(\ell, \dot{\ell}, \ddot{\ell}\); place CM; compute mass map; tip states; \(T_{\mathrm{static}}, T_{\mathrm{dyn}}\); \(\mathbf{H}\).  
3. No ODE solver required for the **prescribed-length** radial model (kinematic).  
4. Optional: compare \(T_{\mathrm{static}}\) vs \(T_{\mathrm{dyn}}\) when \(|\dot{\ell}|,|\ddot{\ell}|\) small.

Tolerances (defaults):

| Check | Rel tol | Abs floor |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| Mass | 0 | 0 |
| \(\|\mathbf{H}(t)-\mathbf{H}(0)\| / \|\mathbf{H}(0)\|\) | 1e-8 | — |
| CM radius drift | 1e-14 | 1e-9 m |

## 14. Limitations (explicit)

1. No libration / skip-rope (θ forced to 0).  
2. No tether elasticity, waves, or shock.  
3. No atmospheric drag on tether.  
4. No electrodynamics.  
5. Tension is an estimate under enforced kinematics — not a validated load case.  
6. Negative tension ⇒ model outside intended regime (slack); not a rope contact simulation.  
7. Reel energy input not attributed to a spacecraft power subsystem budget.
